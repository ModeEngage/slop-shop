# Execute a Research Issue

Answer the assigned questions using facts from the environment or internet. Work without a human in the loop by default.
Draft a recommendation only when the assignment requests one.

## Investigation

Read the assignment and its relevant context. Follow its scope, source, access, environment, and effort constraints.
Investigate assigned questions and supporting questions needed to answer them.

Use available sources and tools independently. If missing access, intent, or a decision prevents an answer, record
the gap and its effect, then continue with independent questions. Do not invent a human decision.

Investigate uncertainty or conflicting evidence when resolving it could materially change an answer. Do not pursue
additional questions that would not affect the assigned answers.

## Evidence and Findings

Provide direct answers, supporting sources or observations, and material uncertainty. Local inspection and experiments
can provide evidence alongside documents. Distinguish observed facts from inferences and assumptions.

When a question cannot be answered, record the findings, the evidence gap, and what would be needed to resolve it.
Do not present an unresolved question as answered. An unsuccessful initial search does not establish that a question
cannot be answered.

Record findings in the location specified by the assignment. If none is specified, return them to the user.
For research assigned through Linear, read [references/linear-findings.md](references/linear-findings.md).

## Completion

Continue research while an assigned question, or a supporting question needed to answer it, remains unresolved and can
be investigated with available sources or tools within the assignment's constraints.

Research is complete when each assigned question has either an evidence-supported answer or a documented explanation
of why it remains unresolved. No unresolved question or evidence conflict that could materially change the assigned
answers can be investigated further within the assignment's constraints. Record the findings before reporting completion.
Completion does not require human confirmation.
